## Installation Guide

Welcome, island architect! Before you start shaping your own pocket-sized paradise, let's get Pokémon Pokopia settled onto your system the right way. This guide assumes you've already secured your copy of the mod build — now we're just here to make sure it takes root smoothly.

### Step 1 — Gather Your Supplies

You'll need a few things before the first brick is laid:

- A clean install of the base game, fully updated to its latest stable version.
- The Pokopia mod package you obtained through the project's official distribution channel.
- A bit of patience and maybe a warm drink — this is a cozy sim, after all.

### Step 2 — Prepare the Soil

Navigate to your game's root directory and locate the dedicated mods folder. If one doesn't exist yet, create it manually — think of it as clearing a plot of land before you build. Keep the folder name exactly as the game expects; a typo here is like planting a tree in the wrong biome.

### Step 3 — Plant the Files

Unpack the Pokopia package and move its contents into that folder. Resist the urge to scatter files across subdirectories — the mod expects its structure intact. Once everything is in place, take a moment to double-check that no stray folders got duplicated during the move.

### Step 4 — Activate the Mod

Launch the game and head into the in-game mod management menu. Toggle Pokopia to the active position and confirm the change. If the game prompts a restart, let it — a fresh load ensures every island feature registers correctly.

### Step 5 — First Steps on the Island

Start a new save (or load an existing one if you're adding Pokopia mid-journey) and look for the island welcome prompt. If it appears, congratulations — your paradise is ready. If not, revisit Step 3; misplaced files are the most common reason for a quiet startup.

### Troubleshooting Tips

- **Mod not showing up?** Confirm the folder structure matches the package exactly.
- **Game crashes on load?** Temporarily disable other mods to rule out conflicts, then re-enable one at a time.
- **Missing island features?** Verify the base game version matches what Pokopia expects.

That's it — your island is yours to shape now. Grab a shovel and a friend, because cozy living starts the moment you step ashore.

## Compatibility Notes

Pokopia is built to dance nicely with a whole island's worth of setups, but a few honest notes will save you a headache before you start planting trees.

**Load Order Is Everything.** Drop Pokopia near the top of your load order so its terrain and building hooks claim their ground before other mods start rearranging the furniture. Anything that reshapes world generation should sit above it — below, and you may find your carefully placed bridges floating in the sea.

**Overlapping Island Overhauls.** Pokopia's construction system touches the same soil as most map-editing mods. If you run another island-shaper alongside it, expect the two to argue over who gets to redecorate. Keep one primary builder per save; your villagers will thank you.

**Interior & Furniture Mods.** This is where Pokopia truly blossoms. Additional furniture packs, decoration kits, and cozy-life enhancers slot in beautifully — just confirm they use the shared item tags so your catalog doesn't split into two stubborn halves.

**UI and Quality-of-Life Tweaks.** Most interface tools play nicely, but ones that rewrite the build menu deserve a second look. If your placement grid suddenly disappears, disable your UI modifier first — it's almost always the culprit.

**Save Compatibility.** Your island persists across updates, but removing Pokopia mid-save can orphan custom structures. Always keep a spare save before a major mod reshuffle — the sea remembers what you built, but the save file may not.

**Performance & Sizing.** Big islands are hungry. If you're stacking many decoration packs, watch your frame pacing in densely built zones; trimming distant object detail helps more than you'd expect.

**Version Matching.** Pokopia checks the game build on launch and warns you if something's out of step. Heed that warning — mismatched versions are the number one cause of mysterious construction glitches.

**A Note for the Tinkerers.** Pokopia ships with an accessible configuration layer and open extension hooks, so you're welcome to build your own additions, expand the catalog, and share your creations with the community. There are advanced options tucked in the config for those who want to push the limits — season to taste, and back up first.

When in doubt: load light, test often, and let your island grow at its own pace.
